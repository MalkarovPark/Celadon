On this page, we publish materials from our scientific work, making them accessible for anyone to explore. These are article texts organized by year and, in some cases, supplemented with presentations that we delivered at related conferences.

Many of the materials overlap with one another, either fully or partially. This reflects the progressive nature of the process of knowledge acquisition, in which new insights emerge from existing ones, enriched through the interpretation of new experience.

It should be noted that the format of presentation here is quite specific and may appear difficult to understand or even unacceptable to some readers. However, this section is not intended for them – it is designed for specialists, for the purposes of factual verification and citation.

In a less formal style, yet offering no less in terms of knowledge, the site’s live articles provide accessible explanations while maintaining a balance between theory and practice.
